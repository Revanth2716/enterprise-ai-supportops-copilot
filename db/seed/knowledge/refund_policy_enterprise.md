# Enterprise Refund & Credit Adjustment Policy

## 1. Scope and Eligibility
This policy governs all monetary refund requests, credit balance adjustments, and invoice cancellations for Enterprise and Mid-Market tier customers.

## 2. Standard Refund Window
Enterprise customers may request a full refund within 30 days of contract activation or renewal if core service capabilities fail to meet agreed technical specifications.

## 3. Duplicate Billing and Erroneous Charges
Charges resulting from technical anomalies, gateway double-submissions, or billing engine misconfigurations are classified as Erroneous Deductions. Erroneous Deductions are 100% refundable immediately, with funds restored to the original payment instrument within 3 to 5 business days, or applied as instant account credit upon customer preference.

## 4. Cancellation and Proration
Mid-term contract terminations are subject to the master service agreement. Monthly seat licenses are non-refundable for partial months unless an unresolved service outage exceeded SLA thresholds.
