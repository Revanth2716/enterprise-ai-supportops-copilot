# Enterprise Service Level Agreement (SLA) Guidelines

## 1. Uptime Guarantees
The Platform provides a 99.95% monthly uptime guarantee for Enterprise Tier accounts and 99.90% for Mid-Market accounts, excluding scheduled maintenance windows announced at least 72 hours in advance.

## 2. Incident Priority Tiers and Response Windows
- **P1 - Critical Outage**: Core infrastructure unavailable or catastrophic data impairment. Initial engineer response within 15 minutes. Hourly status updates required.
- **P2 - Major Degradation**: Severe feature impairment affecting multiple users without complete system stoppage. Initial response within 1 hour.
- **P3 - Standard Request / Invoicing Dispute**: Billing questions, administrative changes, or non-blocking technical queries. Response within 4 business hours.

## 3. SLA Breach Remedies and Credits
If the platform experiences downtime exceeding the monthly SLA allowance:
- 99.0% - 99.95% uptime: 10% monthly service credit.
- 95.0% - 99.0% uptime: 25% monthly service credit.
- Below 95.0% uptime: 50% monthly service credit.
Service credits must be claimed within 30 days of the qualifying incident.
